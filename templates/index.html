How to use

1. Paste your Instagram Reel URL below.

2. Paste your Instagram sessionid cookie (see instructions below).

3. Click Scrape — get comments, replies & profiles as CSV.

How to get your sessionid: Open Instagram in your browser → log in → press ⌘+⌥+I (Mac) or F12 (Windows) → Application tab → Cookies → instagram.com → copy the value of sessionid.
