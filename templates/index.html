How to use

1. Paste your Instagram Reel URL below.

2. Paste your Instagram sessionid cookie.

3. Click Scrape — comments are fetched and auto-categorized.

4. Filter by category, search keywords, or click common comments.

5. Download all or filtered comments as CSV.

Get your sessionid: Open Instagram in browser → log in → press ⌘+⌥+I (Mac) or F12 (Windows) → Application tab → Cookies → instagram.com → copy the value of sessionid.
